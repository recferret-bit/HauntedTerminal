## Declarative Video, Screenshot, Infographic & Audio Vocabulary

- **Zero hardcoded content**: Specs use `{chapter}` and `{level:N}` placeholders (and `{solution:{level:N,count:K}}` steps), resolved at load time from `chapters/<slug>/chapter.manifest.json`.
- **Scene modes (`scenes[].mode`)**:
  - `"play"`: opens `/play/{chapter}/{level:N}?nointro=1`, freezes `window.__stage`, runs `setup[]` commands/waits before `t = 0`, then steps `window.__stage.advance(dtMs)` and executes timed `events[]` (`type`, `submit`, `typeAndSubmit`, `modal`, `skin`, `tick`) while recording `window.__game.events()` timestamps.
  - `"comix"`: opens `/cinematic.html?play=<slug>&t=<from>&muted=1&skippable=0` and interpolates `t` across `[from, to]`.
  - `"storybook"`: opens `/storybook.html?inspect=<model>&ui=0&...` and steps model animation time `[from, to]`.
  - `"laptop"`: opens `/laptop-lab?state=<laptopState>&speed=0&mute=1`.
  - `"page"`: opens any route (`/`, `/comix.html`, `/achievements.html`, `/desktop.html`, `/manual`) with optional deterministic `scrollY: [y0, y1]`.
- **Pure-time Comix Zone overlays (`social-overlay.mjs`)**:
  - Styled after 1995 *Comix Zone* (`credits-art-direction`): `#110e1b` heavy ink borders, hard comic offset shadows, `#ffd166` / `#f4f1ea` paper caption boxes, `#ff6a13` vibecoder orange, `#3ddc84` phosphor green, `#39d0ff` data cyan, `#ff3b8b` malware magenta, `Chakra Petch` + `IBM Plex Mono`.
  - Supports `look: "comix" | "big" | "vertical"`, `crop: "square" | "vertical"`, `bumper`, `captions[]`, `pills[]`, `bursts[]` (comic onomatopoeia), `dialogs[]`, `shots[]`, `card`, and `transition` (`cut`, `flash`, `flash-amber`, `glitch`, `whip`, `zoom`, `dip`, `punch`).
- **Multi-track Audio (`social-ffmpeg.mjs` + `social-audio.mjs`)**:
  - `audio.music`: cue ID in `public/music/` (`title_theme`, `battle_boss`, `cine_intro`, `hub_matrix`, ...) or relative path, with `offset`, `fadeIn`, `fadeOut`, `gainDb`, `loop`.
  - `audio.voiceover`: cue ID in `social/media/specs/audio-cues.json` (when generated in `social/media/out/audio/<id>.mp3`) or file path, optional (`required: false` skips cleanly when not yet generated).
  - `audio.cues[]` & `scenes[].sfx[]`: timed `{ at, cue, gainDb }` entries resolved against `public/sfx/<cue>.mp3`.
  - `audio.autoGameSfx`: mixes timestamped `public/sfx/` cues emitted by `type` keystrokes and `window.__game.events()` during `mode: "play"` capture.
  - New audio via ElevenLabs (`social-media.mjs audio plan <id>... [--generate]`): reuses `audio-gen-cli.mjs` (`CAP = 15000`, `RESERVE = 30000`, Keychain OAuth, dry-run default).
