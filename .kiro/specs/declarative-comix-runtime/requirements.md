# Declarative comix runtime: requirements

The 3D animated Comix engine (`src/cinematic/`) is a generic comic-page renderer and timeline runner. Animated sequences live strictly as connected specs in `comix/<slug>/`, compiled to `src/cinematic/generated/catalog-data.ts`. **Zero TypeScript files in `src/cinematic/` or `src/comix-editor/` may name a sequence slug, page ID, panel ID, or scene-specific role.**

## Requirements

1. **Connected sequence layout.** WHEN loading a sequence from `comix/<slug>/`, THE SYSTEM SHALL organize every sequence cleanly into `README.md`, `comix.manifest.json`, and ordered self-contained `pages/page-XX-<slug>/page.spec.json` files without disconnected horizontal JSON files (`marks.json`, `letters.json`, etc.).
2. **Data-driven stage and cast.** WHEN starting playback for any `ComixManifest`, THE ENGINE SHALL construct the room, backdrop, named 3D landmarks (`points`), static props (`world.props`), animated fixtures (`rain`, `bolt`, `vortex`), and cast actors strictly from manifest asset IDs in `ModelCatalog`.
3. **Relative time & landmark expressions.** WHEN compiling timelines in `src/cinematic/timeline.ts`, THE COMPILER SHALL resolve absolute numbers, `+offset` panel-relative times, and `CUE±offset` expressions (`manifest.cues` merged with `page.cues`) plus named `PointRef` lookups without hardcoded numbers in `src/`.
4. **Generic multi-panel comic pages.** WHEN playing a page timeline, THE RENDERER SHALL reveal comic panels cumulatively within normalized `[x, y, w, h]` landscape/portrait rects, run orthographic `PanelCameraSpec` zooms (`from` -> `to`, `ease`, `portrait`), snapshot frozen panels to 2D textures, and drive 3D `turn` page-flip transitions.
5. **Continuous tracks, discrete beats, and lettering.** WHILE advancing playback from `[0, durationSec]`, THE ENGINE SHALL interpolate `SceneTrack` channels (`actor_visibility`, `actor_motion`, `actor_rig`, `part_visibility`, `material_tint`, `fixture`, `stage_light`, `camera_shake`, `pixelate`), fire `PanelBeat` impulses (`lightning`, `fx`, `fx_repeat`, `arc`, `look`, `state`, `audio`, `key_clicks`), and update `PanelLetter` / `PageOverlay` nodes generically by tagged `kind`.
6. **Declarative skip and end-hold policy.** WHEN the player hits a skip input or reaches `durationSec`, THE RUNNER SHALL consult `manifest.skip` (`"end"` or stepped `SkipRule[]`), `acceptsKeyFrom`, `inputGraceMs`, and `holdAtEnd` rather than hardcoding sequence skip tables.
7. **Deterministic freeze-frame & editor preview.** WHEN `/cinematic.html` runs with `?t=<sec>`, `?draft=1`, or `?editor=1`, THE PREVIEW HOST SHALL step deterministically at `1/60s` (`debug.freezeAt`), read live drafts from `localStorage`, support zoom/pan camera view state (`applySceneView`), tag DOM letters/overlays with `data-comix-*` attributes, and expose 2D/3D hit-testing and marquee selection (`editor-select.ts`).
8. **Zero hardcode & 60fps simulation gates.** WHEN validating sequences with `generate-comix.mjs --verify` or `npm run quality`, THE HARNESS SHALL verify connected layout, catalog asset references, `check-declarative.mjs` isolation, and a full `60fps` headless timeline simulation (`simulateComixPack`) before any release.
