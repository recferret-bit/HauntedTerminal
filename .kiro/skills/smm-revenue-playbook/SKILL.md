## 1. Know the Game Cold (Mood, Hook & Differentiators)

Never write generic "check out our indie game!" copy. Every asset must radiate **Out Of Credits**' exact identity:

- **The 1-Sentence Hook:** Lightning pulls a burned-out developer inside his own PC at 2 AM — every room is a **1995 *Comix Zone*** hardware panel, and his **only weapon is a real Unix terminal and JavaScript laptop**.
- **Mood & Aesthetic:** 1995 Sega *Comix Zone* (heavy `#110e1b` ink frames, paper gutters, yellow caption boxes, Ben-Day halftones, comic SFX bursts) × Gothic Haunted Hardware (attic drives, firewall crypt, RAM hall of mirrors, CPU boiler furnace, cracked kernel core) × dry A-minor 16-bit tracker music leaving crisp space for mechanical keystrokes.
- **Tactile Gameplay Proof:**
  - Real POSIX commands (`ls -la` exposes dotfile spyware, `chmod -x` petrifies Trojans to stone, `ps` + `kill -9 <pid>` calls down lightning, `mv` bridges `/dev/null` gaps) under a tense **30-second turn-based battle clock** (player always acts first; timer holds while coding).
  - Recoverable **clamshell laptop (`item.laptop`)** running real JavaScript in a sandboxed Worker and real HTML/CSS in a sandboxed iframe, plus Dockerfile & Compose repairs.
  - Tactile items: oil **lantern** (`dark` room reveal), hardcover comic **Terminal Field Manual (`F1`)**, live PCB **Motherboard Map (`F3`)**, and **6 terminal skins** (`iTerm2`, `VT100`, `PuTTY`, `Windows Terminal`, `GNOME`, `macOS Terminal`).
  - Satirical cast: cheering **Kiro Ghost**, cigarette-smoking bare-metal **Old Bearded Matrix Coder (`dad.tty`)** rebooting open-weight models (`Qwen`, `DeepSeek`, `Llama`), and the 4 smug parody AI supervisors (**Anti-Gravy**, **Codec**, **Gork**, and **PID 1 Supervisor Clod**).
- **Frictionless Conversion Hook:** All **11 zero-death-verified sectors** of Chapter 1 boot instantly in the browser (`0` install, `0` signup), and players export their `.json` save & **12 `ACH_*` trophies** straight into the offline-verified Tauri v2 + Steamworks desktop edition (Mac, Windows, Linux, Steam Deck).

## 2. Comparable Strategy Rules ($1M Gross Revenue Math)

From `social/research/competitors-and-benchmarks.json`:

1. **Target Math:** `$14.99` Steam base price (`~$11.40` blended gross/unit) -> **~87,720 Year-1 units** -> **55,000 launch-day Steam wishlists** (at `1.6×` Year-1 sales/wishlist) -> cross **7,500+ wishlists BEFORE Steam Next Fest** (Zukowski `r = 0.825` pre-fest velocity rule).
2. **Replicate *The Farmer Was Replaced* ($2.5M–$4.8M) on TikTok & Shorts:**
   - Show **real typed command/code -> immediate 3D visual transformation** within `< 1.5s` (`chmod -x` -> stone Trojan; `kill -9` -> lightning shatter; JS patch -> gate open). Viewers must think: *"Wait, real Linux commands do THAT?!"*
3. **Replicate *Duskers* ($1.6M–$2.5M) & *Buckshot Roulette* ($8M+) Tension:**
   - Frame the **30-second turn timer** + robot voice taunts from **Clod/Gork** as high-stakes duel suspense: *"One typo wastes your turn and PID 1 hits back."*
4. **Replicate *Hacknet* ($3.4M–$8.3M) & *Turing Complete* ($2.1M–$4.1M) Accessibility + Depth:**
   - Reassure non-Linux gamers that Kiro Ghost + the `F1` comic Field Manual teach every command from `ls`, while challenging senior devs with `S`-rank command-count golf and `r/unixporn` terminal skins.
5. **Replicate *Bitburner* (7,400+ reviews) Web-to-Steam Funnel:**
   - Use the instant **Browser Demo** as zero-friction proof on X, Reddit, Hacker News, and YouTube descriptions, always paired with the **Steam Wishlist** CTA + portable `.json` trophy carryover.

## 3. Platform Execution Checklists

### TikTok & YouTube Shorts (`1080×1920` `9:16`, `12–22s`)
- **Frame 0 (`0.0–0.8s`):** Zero logos, zero black fades. Open on a live comic battle room with bold *Comix Zone* caption + starburst (`0 CREDITS!`, `CHMOD -X!`, `30s CLOCK!`).
- **Pacing:** Cut every `3.0–4.2s`; show live `20–22 CPS` keystrokes in xterm + command pills; end with a flash cut that loops seamlessly into Frame 0.
- **Comment Trigger:** End every clip with a concrete terminal question (*"What's your first command against PID 1?"*, *"Team VT100 or Team iTerm2?"*).

### X / Twitter (`1280×720` or `1920×1080` `16:9` + `1:1`, `18–28s`)
- **Muted Autoplay First:** Every X clip must tell the complete story via *Comix Zone* yellow captions and top-left `$ command` pills with sound off.
- **Link & UTM Hygiene:** Keep Post 1 punchy with native `.mp4` attached; place UTM-tagged Browser Demo + Steam Wishlist URLs (`?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<slug>`) in Reply 1 (or Post 1 for X Premium announcements).
- **Weekly Rhythm:** Tue/Thu single-command micro-clips, Wed `#WishlistWednesday` AI-satire lore, Fri 5-part engineering + infographic thread, Sat `#ScreenshotSaturday` skin/room showcase.

### YouTube Long-Form & Steam Trailer (`1920×1080` `16:9`, `45–60s` Trailer + `8–12m` Devlogs)
- **First 3 Seconds:** Lightning strike -> terminal command -> 3D malware hit before any title card.
- **Escalation Arc:** `ls` awakening -> lantern & `chmod -x` battle -> Clamshell Laptop JS/Docker repair -> Dad's Matrix Hub open-weights reboot -> Clod & PID 1 boss -> **Play Free Browser Demo & Wishlist on Steam** end card.
- **Above-the-Fold Conversion:** First two lines of description + pinned comment MUST hold UTM-tracked Steam Wishlist and Browser Demo links.
