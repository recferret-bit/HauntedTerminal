## 1. Executive Summary: Why *Out Of Credits* Has a Real $1M+ Ceiling

Most "coding games" struggle to break $150k because they look like grey spreadsheets. Most "retro stylised dungeon crawlers" drown in a crowded genre because their combat feels identical to 500 other mouse-click indies.

**Out Of Credits** fuses **three proven multi-million-dollar hooks** into one unmistakable 3-second visual read:

1. **Tactile Real CLI + Code Execution (*Hacknet*, *Duskers*, *The Farmer Was Replaced*, *Turing Complete*)**: Every move, door unlock, petrification (`chmod -x`), process kill (`kill -9`), and JS/HTML/CSS/Docker fix is typed in a real Unix terminal or clamshell laptop.
2. **1995 *Comix Zone* + Haunted CRT Hardware (*Inscryption*, *Buckshot Roulette*, *EXAPUNKS*)**: Heavy `#110e1b` ink frames, paper gutters, Ben-Day halftone cel bands, comic onomatopoeia bursts, phosphor-green / data-cyan / hoodie-orange contrast, and dry A-minor tracker music.
3. **Cultural Zeitgeist Satire (Developer Burnout & AI-Credit Parody)**: A developer runs out of credits at night, gets pulled inside his own PC by lightning, teams up with **Kiro Ghost** and a cigarette-smoking bare-metal **Old Bearded Matrix Coder (`dad.tty`)**, reboots open-weight models (`Qwen`, `DeepSeek`, `Llama`), and silences the parody Big Four (**Anti-Gravy**, **Codec**, **Gork**, and **Clod**).

## 4. Platform-Specific Content Strategy (`smm-agent` Operating Rules)

### A. TikTok & YouTube Shorts (`1080x1920` native `9:16`, 12–24 seconds)
**Role:** Top-of-funnel algorithmic discovery (FYP). Zero followers required if frame-0 hook and completion rate win.

1. **The 0.8-Second Frame-0 Rule:** Never open with a logo, black fade, or studio title. Frame 0 MUST show a live comic room with a glowing malware enemy and bold *Comix Zone* yellow caption text posing a challenge or twist:
   - *"POV: You ran out of AI credits at 2 AM and got pulled inside your own PC"*
   - *"In this dungeon crawler your ONLY weapon is real Linux commands"*
   - *"What happens if you type `chmod -x` on a Trojan horse?"*
   - *"30 seconds on the turn timer. One typo and PID 1 wipes your RAM."*
2. **Visual Rhythm (*The Farmer Was Replaced* + *Buckshot Roulette* Formula):**
   - Cut every **2.5–3.8 seconds**.
   - Show the **exact keystrokes** (`$ ls -la`, `$ chmod -x skimmer.sh`, `$ kill -9 1`) in `IBM Plex Mono` pills and the xterm dock, followed within `0.4s` by the 3D cel-shaded comic payoff (hidden `.spycam` revealed by the Tube Transistor, trojan petrified to stone, lightning bolt shattering malware, comic `ZAP!` burst).
3. **Audio Strategy:** Pair dry A-minor tracker beds (`battle_boss`, `title_theme`, `floor_crypt`) with crisp in-game mechanical keystrokes (`key_press`), comic slams (`cine_sfx_slam`), and ElevenLabs robot voice taunts (`taunt_clod_1`, `kiro_advice`) or punchy ElevenLabs narration.
4. **Seamless Loop Ending:** End the final 0.5s with a lightning flash or terminal prompt reset that loops seamlessly back into Frame 0 to push average watch time past 105%.

### B. X / Twitter (`1280x720` or `1920x1080` `16:9` + `1:1` Square, 18–35 seconds)
**Role:** Cult community among software engineers, sysadmins, open-weights AI enthusiasts, and indie game fans; high CTR to Browser Demo & Steam UTM links.

1. **Muted-Autoplay Design:** 80%+ of X scrollers watch muted. Every X video MUST carry crisp *Comix Zone* yellow/paper lower-third captions and top-left command chips so the entire joke and mechanic read silently.
2. **Four Repeatable Content Pillars (5x / week):**
   - **Mechanic Micro-Clips (Tue/Thu):** One Unix command or JS fix -> one dramatic 3D comic reaction (e.g., `mv crate /dev/null`, `chmod -x`, `docker compose up -d`, `skin vt100`).
   - **AI Satire & Lore Drops (Wed `#WishlistWednesday`):** Parody Big Four roasts (**Anti-Gravy**, **Codec**, **Gork**, **Clod**) vs. **Dad (`dad.tty`)** smoking a cigarette at his bare-metal Matrix rack loading open-weight models (`kiro load qwen`).

   - **`#ScreenshotSaturday` Visual Showcase (Sat):** Crisp `1920x1080` stills contrasting terminal skins (`iTerm2`, `VT100`, `GNOME`, `PuTTY`), the hardcover Field Manual, and the PCB Motherboard Map.
3. **Link Placement Discipline:** Put the main narrative + native `.mp4` in Post 1 (no external link throttling on non-thread hooks when testing reach) and place the UTM-tagged Browser Demo + Steam Wishlist link in Reply 1 (or in Post 1 on X Premium long-form launches).

**Role:** Highest wishlist conversion channel (viewers are already on desktop near Steam).

1. **The 50-Second Steam & YouTube Trailer Structure (`youtube-trailer.video.json`):**
