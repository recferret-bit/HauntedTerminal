### Hub fonts

`summary.json`, `hub` at all four sizes: `.SF NS` x6 (example element `<a class="comic-btn teal" href="https://github.com/recferret-bit/HauntedTerminal/releases" ...>`) and `Hiragino Sans` x1 (example `<strong data-va-font="111">⇄ BROWSER ↔ DESKTOP ↔ STEAM SAVE &amp; TROPHY PORTABILITY</strong>`). The glyphs are arrows that the self-hosted Chakra Petch and IBM Plex Mono do not contain. In `pages/index.html` (user-owned):

- line 224 `Follow GitHub Releases ↗`
- line 247 `Download macOS Release ↗`

In `src/` (lead-dev): `src/hub/waitlist.ts` line 185, `btn.textContent = '★ Wishlist on Steam ↗'` (the `★` and `↗`).

The six `.SF NS` glyphs are the `↗` arrows (five in `index.html` plus the one in `waitlist.ts`, which is injected at run time). Menlo came from `code`, `pre` and form controls, which the CSS fix covers. No CSS change can fix an arrow the fonts lack. Choices: replace `↗` with plain text or an ASCII `->`/`>>`, or add the arrow glyph to a bundled font. Lines 224 to 277 and 518 and 284 are the user's.

### The dock (finding 2)

`#shader-dock` is now bottom centre. Death-respawn at 1600x950 (media 2): the strip sits at x 675 to 925, y 530 to 556, clear of the right-hand room cards (they start at x 1297), of `F2` at the left, of `SEGFAULT`, `KERNEL COREDUMP // TRY AGAIN` and `COREDUMP!` (all above y 320). At 1024x768 (death-respawn and level-07), 1280x820 (damage, stats, live level 03) the strip is clear of the cards and `F2`; the card column at 1024 starts at x 810 and the strip ends at x 637. On death-respawn the strip crosses the lightning chain and the hex rain, which is background, not content. Nothing overlaps the damage `-6` lettering (it is at the top). The stats card (stats@1280x820) sits above the strip with a gap of about 20 px.

Why the detector did not flag the old overlap: `#shader-dock` is a watched selector, but the room cards on the right are **not**. They are `.st-ncard` elements (created in `src/renderer/stage-overlay.ts` line 553, `d.className = 'st-ncard ...'`). The watched `.st-card` in `scripts/harness/visual/screens.json` line 8 is the big title card (line 394, `el.className = 'st-card'`, kicker, `h2`, path), a different element. `overlapFlags` (`scripts/harness/lib/visual-detect.mjs`) compares every pair of watched boxes of different selectors (more than 4 x 4 px, skipping ancestor pairs), so it would have flagged `#shader-dock` against `.st-ncard` had that selector been listed. It is a missing selector, not a detector bug. The fix is one string: add `".st-ncard"` to the `play` selector list in `screens.json` line 8 (lead-dev). With it, expect new `overlap` flags only if a real overlap exists; I did not run it that way, since a change to `screens.json` selectors is not mine and would change the flag baseline. Likewise, `F2` is a `<button>` that is not watched, so an overlap with `F2` would be missed too.
