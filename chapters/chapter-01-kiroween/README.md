# Chapter 01 — Kiroween: Kiro Ghost, the Big Four & the Greybeard Father (`chapter-01-kiroween`)

- **Status:** Playable Full-Featured Campaign (`11` executable levels + `6`-page Comix Zone issue)
- **Progression Pattern:** `1 Intro/Tutorial -> 2 Simple Tasks -> Hub 1 -> 2 Tasks -> Hub 2 -> 2 Tasks -> Hub 3 -> Final Boss`
- **Companions:**
  - **`kiro.d` (`prop.kiro_ghost` — Real Kiro Ghost visuals):** White sheet ghost companion who rescues the Vibecoder in a dark room of wires and chips, explains the story of the **Big Four** (`AGY`, `Codex`, `Grok`, `Claude`), teaches `ls`, `mv`, `cat`, `ps`, and `kill`, disables the Big Four one by one as open-weight `.gguf` models come online, and advises in the final boss battle against Claude.
  - **`dad.tty` (`prop.greybeard_rig` — Old Bearded Enterprise Coder):** A cigarette-smoking greybeard hammering bare-metal code at superhuman speed on a Matrix-display CRT server in the Hub. He installs your scavenged RAM + GPU chips to reboot Kiro with open-weight models (`qwen`, `deepseek`, `llama`), and after teleporting back to the real world reveals he is the Vibecoder's **father**!
