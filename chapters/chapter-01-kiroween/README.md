# Chapter 01 — Kiroween: Kiro.d, the Big Four & the Greybeard Father (`chapter-01-kiroween`)

- **Status:** Playable Full-Featured Campaign (`11` executable levels + `6`-page Comix Zone issue)
- **Progression Pattern:** `1 Intro/Tutorial (terminal; basic ls only) -> Level 2 Cache Vault (visible gargoyle drops Tube Transistor on ground -> mv tube_transistor ~ -> try ls -la; SSD disk pickup) -> Level 3 GPU Alley (all enemies hidden -> Tube Transistor ls -la + dotfile riddle) -> Hub 1 (Old Coder gifts map + manual -> ACH_FIELD_MANUAL) -> 2 Tasks (laptop at Level 6) -> Hub 2 -> 2 Tasks -> Hub 3 -> Final Boss`
- **Companions:**
  - **`kiro.d` (`npc.kiro_ghost` — Local Unix `.d` Daemon):** Resident Unix background daemon (`.d`, PID `777`) who rescues the empty-handed Vibecoder in dark wires and chips, explains the whole-chapter mission, delivers his terminal, teaches `ls`, `mv`, `cat`, `ps`, and `kill` (`ls -la` and all `ls` flags stay disabled until the Tube Transistor drops on Level 2), explains RAM (+Max HP) and SSD (auto-Revive) separately on each very first pickup, explains turn-based Big Four minion battles on Level 2, disables the Big Four one by one as open-weight `.gguf` models come online, and advises in the final boss battle against Clod.
  - **`dad.tty` (`npc.greybeard_rig` — Old Bearded Enterprise Coder):** A cigarette-smoking greybeard hammering bare-metal code at superhuman speed on a Matrix-display CRT server in the Hub. On first Hub entry (Level 4) he hands you the PCB Motherboard Map (`map` / `F3`) and Unix Field Manual (`manual` / `F1`, unlocking `/manual.html` and `ACH_FIELD_MANUAL`), installs your scavenged RAM + GPU chips to reboot Kiro.d with open-weight models (`qwen`, `deepseek`, `llama`), and after teleporting back to the real world reveals he is the Vibecoder's **father**!
